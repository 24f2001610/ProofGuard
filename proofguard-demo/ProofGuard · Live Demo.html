This part runs live in your browser: it tests whether two expressions agree at many random points and at special points (0, ±1, 2, 3), so it catches counterexamples and domain changes. Use x, ^, sin, cos, ln, sqrt, exp.

Why this passes the “remove the AI” test

Gemini reads messy, human-written solutions into structured steps and writes the explanation. Rule-based parsers cannot do this for free-form reasoning. Verdicts never come from the LLM: each step is checked by independent engines (algebra, calculus, linear algebra), and anything they cannot confirm is returned as UNVERIFIED instead of guessed.
